Admin authentication

Sign in through the admin workspace or execute POST /api/v1/auth/session below. The browser manages the HttpOnly access and refresh cookies.

Execute GET /api/v1/auth/session, then enter its csrfToken under Authorize → adminCsrf before cookie-authenticated writes. Do not paste cookie values into Swagger UI. The browser supplies Origin and Cookie; an Origin field shown in an operation does not override the browser’s value. This page’s origin is the address in your browser.

Bearer API clients can obtain a token through POST /api/v1/auth/login and use adminBearer. Authorization values stay in memory and clear on reload. Access lasts at most 5 minutes, inactivity at most 15 minutes, and a browser session at most 30 minutes. Swagger UI does not renew sessions or record activity automatically; refresh and sign-out are explicit requests.
